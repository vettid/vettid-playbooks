## What you'll be able to do afterward

- Your phone will require your face or a strong passcode for every sensitive change.
- A thief with your phone in hand can't read your notifications or disable Find My.
- Apps lose the tracking access they never needed.
- If the phone disappears, you can locate, lock, or erase it from any browser.

## The plays

<ol class="plays">
<li>
<strong>Set a six-digit (or better) passcode</strong>
<span class="path">Settings → Face ID &amp; Passcode → Change Passcode → Passcode Options</span>
<p class="did">What this just did: made the passcode — the key to everything else on this list — worth guessing at most ten times.</p>
</li>
<li>
<strong>Turn on Stolen Device Protection</strong>
<span class="path">Settings → Face ID &amp; Passcode → Stolen Device Protection → Turn On Protection</span>
<p class="did">What this just did: critical changes (like disabling Find My or changing your Apple account password) now require Face ID plus a delay when you're away from familiar locations — a stolen passcode alone is no longer enough.</p>
</li>
<li>
<strong>Hide notification previews on the lock screen</strong>
<span class="path">Settings → Notifications → Show Previews → When Unlocked</span>
<p class="did">What this just did: your messages, codes, and reminders no longer display to whoever is holding the phone.</p>
</li>
<li>
<strong>Shorten auto-lock</strong>
<span class="path">Settings → Display &amp; Brightness → Auto-Lock → 1 minute</span>
<p class="did">What this just did: a phone left on a table locks itself before it wanders off unlocked.</p>
</li>
<li>
<strong>Turn off access to controls while locked</strong>
<span class="path">Settings → Face ID &amp; Passcode → scroll to "Allow Access When Locked" → turn off Control Center and USB Accessories</span>
<p class="did">What this just did: a locked phone can no longer be put in airplane mode (which hides it from Find My) or plugged into cracking hardware.</p>
</li>
<li>
<strong>Make sure Find My is fully on</strong>
<span class="path">Settings → [your name] → Find My → Find My iPhone → all three switches on</span>
<p class="did">What this just did: a lost phone is findable, lockable, and erasable from icloud.com/find — even when it's offline or powered off.</p>
</li>
<li>
<strong>Turn off app tracking requests</strong>
<span class="path">Settings → Privacy &amp; Security → Tracking → Allow Apps to Request to Track → off</span>
<p class="did">What this just did: apps can no longer even ask to track you across other companies' apps and websites. That flashlight app is out of the ad business.</p>
</li>
<li>
<strong>Audit the big three permissions</strong>
<span class="path">Settings → Privacy &amp; Security → Location Services / Microphone / Camera — for each, downgrade anything that doesn't obviously need it</span>
<p class="did">What this just did: cut standing access to your location, mic, and camera down to the apps with a real reason. When in doubt: "While Using" beats "Always," and "Never" beats both.</p>
</li>
</ol>

## You're now covered against…

- **A stolen or lost phone** — locked fast, protected against passcode-thieves, findable and erasable from anywhere.
- **Shoulder-surfers and snoops** — nothing sensitive on the lock screen, no controls accessible while locked.
- **The easy tracking** — cross-app tracking requests silenced and permissions cut back to the apps that earn them.

Fifteen minutes. No apps installed, nothing to pay for, nothing that breaks
tomorrow. That's a warm-up.
